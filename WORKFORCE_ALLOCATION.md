# Workforce allocation

The application currently has configurable registered roles, explicit owner-assigned specialist document work, approved fixed planning tasks, employee tool/routing caps and persisted invocation state. General meetings can create at most four selected-participant document follow-ups within their original job cap. These operations are not requirements-based autonomous staffing.

Milestone C must generate and version team/task proposals from requirements, role skills and budget/capacity; exact owner approval must precede allocation. Add distributed capacity/concurrency controls, dependencies, budget-aware scheduling and visible assignment/capacity evidence. A registered/enabled employee must never be counted as active live inference without a real call and valid worker lease.
